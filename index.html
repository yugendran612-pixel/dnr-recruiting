About DNR

Scheduled to officially launch in Q1 2027, Digital Native Recruiting (DNR) is established with a singular, powerful vision: to construct a seamless, high-quality manpower pipeline between India and Singapore.

Singapore's rapid digital transformation requires a consistent influx of highly skilled professionals. Simultaneously, mainland India possesses one of the most vibrant and fast-growing pools of digital talent in the world. DNR exists to bridge this gap.

"For a brighter Singapore and India."

Our "digital native" methodology means we rely on modern vetting, continuous developer education, and remote-first assessment protocols. We don't just recruit; we develop and prepare talent to thrive in Singapore's unique corporate ecosystem.
